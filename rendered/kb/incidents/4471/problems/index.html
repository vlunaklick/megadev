What each one is

  • parser: the shared parser reads month first, and every tenant on file writes the day first. Day and month swap: 7 April becomes 4 July. Any channel.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. Day-first tenants type month first because the form tells them to, and the form reads it day first. Only the form channel, only booking-v3 tenants. Fixing the parser does not fix this.
  • shift: the date moves by one day, not day ↔ month. “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” That points at a time zone conversion. A month-first parser cannot do this. The desk noted these as a one-day move (desk-notes.json), but causes.ts files them under parser by tenant, so the #4471 table counts them there.
